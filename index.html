About Me

Welcome to my personal portfolio! I am a dedicated student of Spanish and English languages, focusing on their application as foreign or second languages. My studies revolve around understanding linguistic structures, language acquisition theories, and developing effective teaching methodologies for diverse learners.

My goal is to create engaging, culturally rich, and communicative learning experiences that bridge linguistic gaps and foster global understanding.
